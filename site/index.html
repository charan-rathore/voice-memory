        Where it stands

        Built for the Bolna Symphony Buildathon, running on fictional records.

        Works now

        • Typed questions on this page, straight from the evidence (voice calling is paused)
        • Amended, preliminary, proposed, superseded and still-open values, tracked per value
        • Lab results compared across reports, refusing when units or patients differ
        • Short spoken answers built from the record, with the cited line on the original page
        • PDF, Word, scanned image and text ingestion with page-anchored citations
        • Lab explanations in English, Hindi or Hinglish, in the language you ask
        • A memory graph of documents, values and the sentences behind them
        • Your own documents on this page, in a private workspace deleted after 30 idle minutes
        • A key-free API for scripts and agents (llms.txt, OpenAPI)

        Next

        • Paraphrase-aware search: the dense retriever finds the right passage, the evidence gate doesn't accept it yet (measured)
        • A benchmark of real, messy records written by people outside the team
        • Clinician review of lab explanations before any patient pilot
        • Phone-number calls and WhatsApp voice notes through Bolna
